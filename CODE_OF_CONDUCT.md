# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Six Refuses is a learning project built around curiosity, rigor, and the freedom
to question assumptions. We commit to making participation welcoming regardless
of age, experience, identity, disability, nationality, appearance, religion, or
technical background.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind, patient, and specific.
- Critique claims and implementations, not people.
- Welcome sincere questions, including foundational ones.
- Distinguish uncertainty from incompetence.
- Give sources for scientific corrections.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person’s personal information.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and repair harm when mistakes happen.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
misinformation, sustained disruption, personal attacks, doxxing, and other
conduct that makes participation unsafe are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews,
and project-affiliated spaces. Report conduct concerns privately to the
repository maintainers through their GitHub profiles. Maintainers may edit or
remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Reports will be handled as confidentially and promptly as practical. Good-faith
reports will not result in retaliation.
